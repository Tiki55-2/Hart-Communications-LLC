NoteWhy fixed price
A note on how this works

Full-service consultants are worth it — for the campaigns that can afford them. Most down-ballot races can't, so they go without research, run amateur email programs, and sign media plans no one has pressure-tested.

This firm exists to close that gap. Modern research and production tools have cut the hours these deliverables take. Instead of billing for the old hours, the savings are passed through as pricing a legislative or local race can actually budget.

The tools do the assembly. The judgment — what's salient in your district, what survives scrutiny, what actually moves your voters — is the product.
