File 02The 70% rule

At least 70% of your budget goes to paid media

Voters never see your overhead. They see your ads, your mail and your streaming spots. We believe 70% or more of a campaign's budget should go directly into paid media, and we build every budget to that mark.

NoteWhy boutique
A note on how this works

Big firms come with big overhead, and campaigns end up paying for it. Every dollar that goes to a consultant's overhead is a dollar voters never see.

We stay small so the math runs the other way. The budget goes to paid media first, and our fee is spelled out before you sign.

What you're paying for is judgment: what matters in your district, what holds up under scrutiny and what actually moves your voters.
